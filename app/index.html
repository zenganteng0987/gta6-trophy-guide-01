⚠ 1 potentially missable trophy

Positive Mental Attitude (Bronze) — meditate at every spot across Leonida. During Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia meditates on the balcony at Jason's house: follow her and accept. That moment is not marked as a meditation spot, it disappears when Chapter 1 ends, and guides disagree on whether it counts — do it to be safe. Every other trophy can still be earned after the story. Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough, no online trophies, nothing else missable. Estimated total ≈ 200 hours, difficulty 4/10. Cheats disable trophies and saving, and there is no mission replay — keep a manual save at the start of each chapter (15 manual slots). Roadmap source: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap.
