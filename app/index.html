Roadmap to the platinum

PowerPyx rates the platinum at about 200 hours and difficulty 4 out of 10, in one playthrough. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Only one trophy may be missable: Positive Mental Attitude.

      About this guide

      Names, descriptions and grades are the official list as PlayStation publishes it. Every guide is written for this entry, in our own words, and names its sources beside the trophy. Where no unlock method is confirmed at launch, the guide says so honestly instead of guessing. Trophy facts come from PowerPyx first, with Output Lag, CryptoGames, EGamers, GTA Zone, GTASpotter and Push Square as supporting sources.

      Five trophies have no confirmed trigger yet at launch: In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin. Their guides carry the unconfirmed leads and say so plainly.
